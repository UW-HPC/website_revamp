
# Tillicum

UW students may apply for Tillicum computing credit grants to support research, coursework, professional development, and exploratory projects that benefit from advanced GPU computing.

Tillicum is a GPU-accelerated research cluster featuring NVIDIA H200 GPUs with 141 GB of GPU memory and high-bandwidth interconnects. These resources support computationally intensive workloads that require high-capacity GPUs, parallel computing across multiple GPUs, or scaling across multiple nodes.

## GPU Credits for Students

The program offers two tiers of computing credits:

**Tier 1 — Initial allocation:** $250 in computing credits for eligible UW students starting a project on Tillicum. Applications will be assessed according to need and project readiness to transition to Tillicum.

**Tier 2 — Additional allocation:** Additional computing credits for students who have made progress using their initial allocation and can demonstrate continued technical need and a clear plan for completing their work.

* Tier 2 applications must demonstrate that at least 90% of the initial $250 allocation has been used and provide preliminary results from that allocation.
* Tier 2 applicants must provide the requested technical justification and project information.

* Current Tier 2 maximum award: **$500** *(award amounts may change depending on available funding)*

Credits are intended to support projects that can benefit from Tillicum's GPU architecture. Exploratory and learning-focused projects are welcome, although priority will be given to projects that demonstrate meaningful use of these capabilities.

* Who is eligible? **UW students paying the Student Technology Fee**

### Applying for Access

Before applying for Tillicum credits or using RCC resources, you must [join RCC](https://depts.washington.edu/uwrcc/#joining-rcc):

1. Join RCC on [Husky Link](https://huskylink.washington.edu/rcc/club_signup)
2. Join the [RCC Slack Workspace](https://join.slack.com/t/uw-rcc/shared_invite/zt-2l7ab48ar-S_SbfLiCsdpF~ofbCR0yIg)
3. Join the [RCC email list](http://mailman12.u.washington.edu/mailman/listinfo/hpc-list)
4. Submit your [application](https://uwconnect.uw.edu/it?id=sc_cat_item&sys_id=6faf5039870a72906f1997dd3fbb355c)

### Application Review Timeline

* Applications will be processed on a rolling basis.
* You will be notified of your application acceptance via email.
* Most applications are processed within 5-7 business days.

### Program Terms

* Awarded Tillicum credits must be used within **6 months of account access**.
* Project [storage](./tillicum_storage.md) on Tillicum is temporary. **Students are responsible for removing any data they wish to keep before their project ends.** Data remaining after the project period may be deleted.

### Not sure if Tillicum is right for you?

If you're not sure whether your project is a good fit or you have questions about your eligibility, [reach out to the UWIT Research Computing Team for a consultation](https://uwconnect.uw.edu/sp?id=sc_cat_item&sys_id=47d23c5d87bd6a50e385333e3fbb356b) about Tillicum.
